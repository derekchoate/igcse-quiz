A robot isn't defined by looking human, or by seeming clever — it's defined by three checkable things, all at once: a mechanical structure or framework, electrical components such as sensors, a microprocessor and actuators, and being programmable. Miss even one of the three and it isn't a robot, whatever else it does. Sort each machine below onto the pile it belongs on, using exactly that check.

Check the three off one at a time for each machine: does it have a physical body or framework? Does it have sensors, a microprocessor and actuators built in? Can it be reprogrammed to behave differently?
The remote-control car has a body and a motor, but every single movement is a person's thumb, live, right now — that's not a stored program running on its own, so it's missing the third piece.
